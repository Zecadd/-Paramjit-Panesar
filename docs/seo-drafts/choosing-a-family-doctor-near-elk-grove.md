## What would repeated trips look like?

### If you need a physical or a vaccine

A routine checkup may include a physical exam or screening. If you also want to discuss a vaccine, ask each office whether it can address both at one appointment. Enter a second trip only if the office says you would need one. [MedlinePlus on health checkups](https://medlineplus.gov/healthcheckup.html)

### If you expect follow-up for diabetes or blood pressure

If you have diabetes, your care team can help you decide how often you need an A1C test and what goal fits you. Ask where the test would take place and whether you would need another trip to discuss the result. [NIDDK on managing diabetes](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/managing-diabetes)

If you already measure your blood pressure at home, ask each clinician how to share the readings and who responds. Agree with your clinician on when to measure and what to do about readings outside your individual goals. [AAFP on checking blood pressure at home](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2021/0900/p237-s1)

## Which office fits my week and my plan?

### Verify the clinician and the address

Give your insurer both doctors' names and the addresses in the worksheet. Ask if your plan covers visits at each site and whether you must select the doctor as your primary care physician. To [confirm insurance at Apollo](https://www.apollophysicians.com/insurance-accepted), have your exact plan name ready. For questions about credentials or who follows up on results, use the [Sacramento physician comparison checklist](https://www.apollophysicians.com/choosing-a-family-doctor-sacramento).

### Add up the time you expect to be away

Write down the visits your care team actually anticipates. Add the door-to-door time for each confirmed in-person trip, then mark any visit whose format is still undecided. A shorter drive may still be hard to arrange if the only appointment offered falls outside your driver's schedule.

## Questions before deciding

### How do I compare offices if I depend on a ride?

Ask both offices for an appointment time you could actually take. Ask your driver when they could drop you off and pick you up at each address. Compare the hours away for both of you, including the return trip.

### Who can confirm which follow-ups would mean another trip?

Ask the clinician or care team which parts of your follow-up need an exam, a test or a conversation. The scheduling team can confirm the location and available visit format once it knows what you need.

If the South Sacramento trip fits your week, read about [becoming a new patient](https://www.apollophysicians.com/new-patients) or call Apollo Physicians at [916-236-5800](tel:+19162365800) to ask about openings.
