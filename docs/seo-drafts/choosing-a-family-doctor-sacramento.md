## Can I choose this Sacramento physician under my plan?

Check the exact plan name on your insurance card. Ask member services to look up the doctor at the street address where you would see them.

### Match the clinician, medical group and office address

For Apollo, you can [check Apollo's accepted insurance plans](https://www.apollophysicians.com/insurance-accepted), then ask your insurer, "Can I select Dr. Panesar at 8191 Timberlake Way, Suite 200, under this plan?" If a medical group appears on your card, give its name too. Write down the answer and date. Call the office to confirm what it can schedule and ask what you would pay.

### Verify a Nivano or Medi-Cal PCP assignment

If Nivano appears on your card or in your plan directory, read how to [verify a Nivano PCP assignment](https://www.apollophysicians.com/nivano-physicians-doctor-sacramento). Ask which doctor you can select as your primary care physician, or PCP.

If you have Medi-Cal, review the steps for [choosing a Medi-Cal primary doctor](https://www.apollophysicians.com/medi-cal-doctor-sacramento). Ask your health plan which doctors you can choose and whether any change needs to take effect before your appointment. Ask the office to confirm your specific plan before you travel.

## How can I check training and continuity?

Check the clinician's credentials, then ask how the practice handles results, repeat visits and care when that clinician is away.

### Specialty, board certification and language needs

Apollo's profile lists Dr. Paramjit Panesar, MD, as board-certified in family medicine. Review [Dr. Panesar's credentials and training](https://www.apollophysicians.com/dr-paramjit-panesar-md), then check the issuing board's record for his current status.

Tell each practice which ages you need care for and which language you prefer for medical conversations. Ask who would see you and whether you can request the same clinician for future visits.

### Who follows results and covers absences?

Ask how the practice shares test results, whom you should contact with a question and who covers when your usual clinician is away.

For preventive care, ask how the clinician decides which screenings to discuss and how you will hear about the results. Preventive visits can include screening tests to look for disease early, vaccinations and health counseling. [CDC preventive care guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-disease/prevention/preventive-care.html)

If you have diabetes, ask how the practice plans follow-up and reviews your results. The A1C test reflects average blood glucose over about three months. Your care team can help set your A1C goal and how often to test. [NIH/NIDDK diabetes guidance](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/managing-diabetes)

## Questions before choosing a family physician

### How should I use reviews alongside credentials?

Use reviews to decide what to ask about communication, waits and office contact. Verify the clinician's training and certification separately, and ask how the practice would handle your own care needs.

### How can I confirm I can schedule with the clinician I chose?

Call the office and ask whether that clinician is accepting new patients with your specific plan and what appointment options are currently available. For Apollo, call 916-236-5800 and read about [new patient appointments at Apollo](https://www.apollophysicians.com/new-patients) before booking.

### Which address should I give my insurer if I live in Elk Grove?

For an in-person visit with Dr. Panesar at Apollo, give your insurer 8191 Timberlake Way, Suite 200, Sacramento, CA 95823. If you are considering a doctor in Elk Grove, ask your plan to check that doctor's own office address separately.
